## Website Features
- Filter bar by subspecialty: General, Cardiac, Pediatric, Regional/Pain, Critical Care, Trauma, Education, Technology, Ambulatory, Resident-Focused
- "Open Now" filter, status badges, submission links, and alert banner derive their state from saved opening and closing dates. They refresh on page load, every 30 seconds, and when the page regains focus or visibility. Confirmed future openings show NOT YET OPEN; unconfirmed calls remain TBD.
- Alert banner at top for open deadlines with animated indicator
- Status badges: OPEN (green), NOT YET OPEN / TBD (blue), CLOSED (gray). An expired award-only cutoff asks readers to verify general submissions.
- Past cycle section for year-ahead planning
- Print-friendly CSS for PDF export
- **Left sidebar calendar** — mini monthly calendars from Jul 2026 through Sep 2027; includes conferences and dated abstract deadlines in that range across academic years; conference dates filled in conference color, abstract deadline days marked with a red dot; color legend below
- **Clickable calendar dates** — clicking a highlighted date switches to Cards view, scrolls to the conference card, and flashes a 3.5s teal glow highlight around it; active filters that would hide the card are cleared automatically
- **List by Date view** — toggle between Cards and List views; list is sorted chronologically with academic year section headers, all filters apply to both views
- **Single JS data source** — all conference data lives in one `CONFERENCES` array; calendar, list, and cards all render from it so nothing gets out of sync

## Maintenance
- **Recommended update cadence:** Each July 1, plus a September check (many fall deadlines land Sept–Dec)
- **Live site:** https://chinng1.github.io/anesthesia-conferences/ (GitHub Pages, repo `chinng1/anesthesia-conferences`). Every push to `main` republishes it within about a minute.
- **Monthly check:** `.github/workflows/monthly-check.yml` runs on the 1st of each month (7–8 AM Pacific). It runs the tests and `check_conferences.cjs`, then opens a GitHub issue listing open calls, deadlines within 6 weeks, finished meetings, TBD calls for meetings within 9 months, and broken links. It never edits `index.html`. Run it any time from the repo's Actions tab, or locally with `node check_conferences.cjs` (`--no-links` for a fast run, `--today YYYY-MM-DD` to preview a date).
- **How to update:** Ask Claude to work through the latest check issue and re-search society websites, edit `index.html`, update "Last updated", then commit and push.

### Automatic status maintenance

- `abstractStatus` is the last verified status; do not edit it just to advance the clock.
- Add `abstractOpensOn: [year, month, day]` for a confirmed opening date, or `abstractOpensAt` with an explicit ISO UTC offset for a timed opening. ASA scientific abstracts open January 5, 2027; SCA opens September 15, 2026.
- `abstractConfirmedOpenOn` records the date a call was verified open when its original opening date is unknown.
- Use `abstractClosesAt` with an explicit ISO UTC offset for published cutoff times. The SPA source explicitly says noon EST; CCAS says noon Eastern Time (EDT on October 26). These are preserved as published.
- Date-only deadlines stay open through the listed date in `abstractTimeZone`, defaulting to America/Los_Angeles. No exact closing time is implied by this fallback.
- `abstractDeadlineKind: 'award'` prevents an award eligibility deadline from being mistaken for a general submission closure.
- This is local date logic, not an automatic website check. Newly announced calls and changed deadlines still require source updates.
- Run `node --test tests/abstract-status.test.cjs` to check opening/closing boundaries, time zones, and refresh/filter behavior.
